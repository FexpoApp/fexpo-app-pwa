# Pósteres por categoría

Arte generado para ilustrar rubros, sin logotipos ni productos atribuidos a empresas reales. Imagen maestra en design-sources/categories-atlas.png; entrega web en public/assets/posters/categories-atlas.webp. Solo se convirtió el formato de la entrega, sin retoque posterior. Cada celda se muestra con CSS según su categoría.

## Prompt de producción

Use case: ads-marketing.
Asset type: a SINGLE 3-column by 2-row image atlas of six cinematic category cover artworks for the Fexpo mobile exhibitor catalog. This is one sprite-sheet asset, not six deliverables.
Create exactly six edge-to-edge rectangular panels with equal dimensions, NO margins, NO gaps, NO borders, NO labels, NO typography. Overall near-square 9:8 proportion; each panel is a 3:4 portrait cover. Subjects centered with safe crops.
Order is essential: top left TECHNOLOGY — sophisticated electronic processor and blue-lit robotic components, deep navy and lime reflections; top middle HEALTH — elegant modern medical scanner and clean clinical equipment, muted ivory and navy, no patients; top right FINANCE — architectural bank columns with small metal coins, premium navy and warm silver, no currency text.
Bottom left GASTRONOMY — beautifully lit Bolivian-inspired coffee, cacao and quinoa culinary ingredients, warm natural textures, no brands; bottom middle EDUCATION — an open architectural library, books and luminous study desk, navy and ivory, no people; bottom right STARTUPS — precision agricultural sensor among green leaves, technology and small-business innovation, active lime and dark green.
Shared style: high-end editorial photographic art direction, dramatic but natural light, detailed tangible real objects, rich clear compositions reminiscent of streaming app poster covers. Strong focal object in every panel, not a generic icon, not 3D clay. Brand palette anchors: navy #132D46, ivory #F5F4EF, lime #D5F24A. Distinct relevant imagery for each subject; one visual grammar. No made-up companies, company logos, endorsements, slogans, numbers, movie titles, watermarks or human faces. These are illustrative category covers; actual company names will be placed separately in accessible HTML.
